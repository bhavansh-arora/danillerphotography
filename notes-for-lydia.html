9upgrades

Hi Lydia! Your current site has a lovely personal voice. We kept every bit of it and rebuilt the experience around one goal: turning quiet City Hall browsers into booked dates.

Below is each upgrade, where to see it, and why it matters for your enquiries. Everything is live in this preview; click around.

Start with City HallSee the homepage
The City Hall booking journeyUpgrades 01–05
01

Interactive collection builder

Couples tap I, II, III or IV and instantly see images, investment, time and what’s included, with matching photos. One tap books that exact collection.

Why it helps: City Hall couples plan fast and compare on price and time. Letting them self-select means fewer “what’s included?” emails and warmer, ready-to-book enquiries.

See it live
02

A smarter enquiry flow

Four short steps with a live summary and estimate. The date, collection and guest count chosen anywhere on the site carry straight in.

Why it helps: One question per screen feels effortless on a phone, and every enquiry reaches you with the date, collection, guests and location already filled in.

Try it pre-filled
03

An insider’s map of City Hall

Six signature spots (the gilded doors, Grand Staircase, Mayor’s Balcony and more), each with your photo and an insider tip.

Why it helps: Your 100+ ceremonies are your biggest advantage. This puts that expertise on show, and gives Google rich, specific content for “SF City Hall wedding photographer” searches.

See the map
04

“Your City Hall day” timeline

A sample 90-minute schedule, from hello on the steps to bubbly, including how you guide them through check-in.

Why it helps: The unknowns of check-in and timing are what make couples hesitate. Showing the day removes that worry before they ever email.

See the timeline
05

A filterable City Hall gallery

28 images sorted by Ceremony, Portraits, Guests, Details and Outside, with a full-screen viewer that swipes on mobile.

Why it helps: Couples look for proof that matches their plans (“will our 10 guests look good?”). Filters get them there in one tap.

Open the gallery
→

The result

A couple can land on City Hall, choose a collection, see the spots, understand the day, and send a complete enquiry in about three minutes, without writing a single email from scratch.

Across the whole siteUpgrades 06–09
06

“Check your date” is always one tap away

In the header, a sticky bar on mobile, and quick date-check forms on the home, City Hall and Family pages.

Why it helps: Most couples browse on their phones. The next step should always be within thumb reach.

07

Reviews where decisions happen

Your five testimonials now sit next to pricing and booking points, plus a dedicated Kind Words page with highlights.

Why it helps: “She knew all the picture-perfect spots inside SF City Hall” does more selling beside the collections than on a separate page.

08

Fast, findable, accessible

Optimised WebP images in two sizes, descriptive titles and meta for every page, alt text on every photo, keyboard-friendly menus and galleries, and reduced-motion support.

Why it helps: Faster pages keep mobile visitors around, and clear page titles help you show up for City Hall, Bay Area wedding and family searches.

09

Your voice, louder

The invisibility cloak, the stuffed pigeon, “pucker up, buttercup”, dancing with grandma: your own words, given the stage they deserve.

Why it helps: Couples book you because you feel like a good friend. The design leans into that playful warmth instead of hiding it.
