You’ll receive your images 4–8 weeks after your wedding, depending on the season. The editing process takes time, as I enhance each image individually and only present the best. I average around 75 images an hour, so for a 6-hour wedding you can expect around 450 images or more.

I’ll lightly edit and enhance your whole batch of images so they look their best, while keeping a natural aesthetic. I’ll remove distracting elements like the random cilantro leaf between your teeth. For more extensive retouching, I’ll wait to hear which photos are your favourites and retouch those as needed. Most often the images don’t need extra attention.
