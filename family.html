(01) — How it feelsLess posing, more playing

A sense of play, a little silliness.

I’m a proud auntie of three and spent years as a camp counselor (second-best job ever, after photographer!). I bring that same energy to every family session, to create authentic smiles all around.

  • Out exploring. The beach, a park, a favourite trail, the Muni.
  • At home. Where little ones feel most like themselves.
  • Every age. Bumps, newborns, toddlers, teens and grandparents.
  • Pets welcome. Dogs, cats, and very patient chickens.
