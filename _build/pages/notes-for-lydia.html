Hi Lydia! Your current site has a lovely personal voice. We kept every bit of it and rebuilt the experience around one goal: turning quiet City Hall browsers into booked dates.

Below is each upgrade, where to see it, and why it matters for your enquiries. Everything is live in this preview. Prefer a guided tour? Turn on notes mode and numbered pins explain each feature right on the page.

The City Hall booking journeyUpgrades 01–05
01

Interactive collection builder

Couples tap I, II, III or IV and instantly see images, investment, time and what’s included, with matching photos. One tap books that exact collection.

Why it helps: City Hall couples plan fast and compare on price and time. Letting them self-select means fewer “what’s included?” emails and warmer, ready-to-book enquiries.

See it live
02

An insider’s map of City Hall

A hand-drawn sketch of the building that draws itself in, with your six favourite spots pinned on it. Tap a pin to see your photo from that spot and an insider tip.

Why it helps: Your 100+ ceremonies are your biggest advantage. The map makes that expertise visible in seconds, and gives Google rich, specific content for “SF City Hall wedding photographer” searches.

See the map
03

A City Hall day planner, timed to sunset

Couples pick their date, ceremony time and collection and get a personal timeline, with that evening’s sunset and golden hour worked out for San Francisco.

Why it helps: Check-in and timing are what make couples hesitate. The planner answers both, and the whole plan arrives with their enquiry, so you know their timing before you reply.

Try the planner
04

An enquiry that already knows the answers

Four short steps with a live summary and estimate. Anything chosen elsewhere on the site (date, collection, guests, ceremony time, hours, playground) is already filled in, along with which tool they used.

Why it helps: One question per screen feels effortless on a phone, and every enquiry reaches you complete: no back-and-forth to find out the basics.

Try it pre-filled
05

A filterable City Hall gallery

28 images sorted by Ceremony, Portraits, Guests, Details and Outside, with a full-screen viewer that swipes on phones. The first twelve show straight away to keep the page quick.

Why it helps: Couples look for proof that matches their plans (“will our 10 guests look good?”). Filters get them there in one tap.

Open the gallery

The result

A couple can land on City Hall, choose a collection, see the spots, plan their day around the light, and send a complete enquiry in about three minutes, without writing a single email from scratch.

Across the whole siteUpgrades 06–11
06

“Check your date” is always one tap away

In the header, a sticky bar on phones, and quick date checks on the home and City Hall pages. Picking a date shows the day, how many days to go and that evening’s sunset.

Why it helps: Most couples browse on their phones. The next step is always within thumb reach, and the instant feedback makes it feel personal.

See the date check
07

A coverage sizer for weddings

Drag to choose hours and watch the image count build, using your own figure of about 75 edited images an hour.

Why it helps: “How many photos will we get?” is answered before they ask, and the hours they choose travel into the enquiry.

Try the sizer
08

A playdate planner for families

Families pick a playground (by the bay, the park, around the city or at home) and a date, and see a mini moodboard from your galleries, your words, and when the soft light starts.

Why it helps: It turns “where should we shoot?” into an exciting first step, and you get their choice of spot with the enquiry.

Plan a playdate
09

Reviews where decisions happen

Your testimonials sit next to pricing and booking points, plus a dedicated Kind Words page with highlights.

Why it helps: “She knew all the picture-perfect spots inside SF City Hall” does more selling beside the collections than on a separate page.

10

Page changes that feel like an app

Moving between pages, a panel carrying the next page’s name sweeps across while the new page loads behind it. Back and forward buttons, shared links and bookmarks all still work.

Why it helps: It feels polished, and it removes the jumpy flash some iPhone browsers show when a new page loads.

11

Fast, findable, shareable

Fonts and images served from the site itself, descriptive titles for every page, a share image for each page, your business details and FAQs in the format Google reads, and a sitemap.

Why it helps: Faster pages keep mobile visitors around, links look great when shared by text or on social, and clear page data helps you show up for City Hall, Bay Area wedding and family searches.
