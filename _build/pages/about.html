{{img lydia-cats|Lydia sitting between two painted cats on a wall|sizes="(max-width: 900px) 50vw, 24vw"}}

Caught smiling at my own screen.

There are times when I’m editing shots from a wedding and I start smiling to myself, because the joy is so palpable. I’ve even caught myself pursing my lips at the computer screen because someone is smooching in the image.

That’s the feeling I want you to have, every time you open your gallery. Twenty years from now, too.
