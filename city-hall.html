All the magic. None of the stress.

    (03) — An insider’s mapSix spots I’ll take you

    Every corner, known by heart.

    An insider’s sketch · not to scale · tap a pin
    01 · Exterior

    Your first steps out as newlyweds.

    Gold leaf, wrought iron and the City Hall sign overhead. It’s the shot that says “we did it”.

    Insider tipExit together, slowly. I’ll be waiting outside to catch the burst of joy.
    02 · Rotunda

    The marble sweep everyone dreams of.

    The Grand Staircase is the heart of the building. Shot from above, it turns your dress into a painting.

    Insider tipLong trains look incredible here. I’ll fluff it, you just look at each other.
    03 · Ceremony

    Held in a circle by your people.

    There’s something about reciting vows surrounded by close friends and family, with the rotunda glowing behind you.

    Insider tipTell me who your VIPs are beforehand so I can catch every reaction.
    04 · Upper floors

    Arches, soft light, fewer crowds.

    Ted and Raz flew in from Hong Kong to marry here. It was hard not to jump for joy along with them.

    Insider tipThe upper galleries are my secret weapon for relaxed, crowd-free portraits.
    05 · Window light

    Natural light, made for portraits.

    Tall lattice windows pour soft light into the corridors. Perfect for quiet, close-up moments.

    Insider tipAfternoon light is magic here. Ask me about the best times when you book.
    06 · Outside

    Confetti, fist bumps and the whole crew.

    Group photos with the dome behind you, then a little chaos to send you off.

    Insider tipBring bubbles or a bouquet toss. Kids (and chihuahuas) steal the show here.
